# AD_SD_CBT_practice

CBT practice for the ITUSD-1-1-3 Associate Diploma in Software Development.
Full and quick mock exams that follow the official unit blueprint (60 questions, 2 hours)
A syllabus checklist of all 122 official knowledge topics, with key facts and study references
A review deck: wrong or guessed answers return until answered correctly three times
After every answer, a short note on why each option is right or wrong, and the unit the question comes from
A bilingual vocabulary trainer: 286 terms, each with a short definition in English and Arabic
An 8-week study plan to the January 2027 exam
The trainer is one self-contained file, index.html. It needs no server, build step or database.
